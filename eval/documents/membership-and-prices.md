# Kestrelwood Coworking: memberships and prices

All prices are invented for testing and include VAT.

## Day pass

A day pass costs 350 lira and gives access to any free hot desk from opening until 18:00. Day passes are sold at the front desk and online.

## Flex membership

Flex membership costs 2,900 lira per month and includes ten days of hot desk use per calendar month. Unused days do not carry over to the next month.

## Resident desk

A resident desk costs 6,500 lira per month. You get your own desk, a lockable drawer, and access around the clock with your key card.

## Private office

Private offices for two to six people start at 18,000 lira per month. Prices depend on size and on the floor.

## Discounts

Students get 30 percent off the flex membership with a valid student card. Paying for a year in advance gives two months free on any membership.

## Cancelling

Memberships renew every month. To cancel, tell the front desk or email the team at least 14 days before your next billing date. Day passes cannot be refunded.

## Payment

We accept card payments and bank transfers. Invoices are emailed on the first working day of each month.
