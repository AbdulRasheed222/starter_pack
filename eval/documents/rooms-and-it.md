# Kestrelwood Coworking: meeting rooms, internet and printing

## Meeting rooms

There are three meeting rooms: Heron seats four people, Osprey seats eight, and Albatross seats sixteen and has a projector. Book them in the member app. Resident and private office members get eight hours of meeting room time per month included; extra hours cost 400 lira per hour. Cancel a booking at least two hours ahead, or the time is still counted.

## Wi-Fi

The members' network is called Kestrelwood-Members and the password is printed on the back of your key card. Guests use the Kestrelwood-Guest network, which needs no password but is limited to 20 megabits per second. If the internet is down, report it in the member app under "Problems".

## Printing

Each member gets 100 free black-and-white pages per month. After that, a black-and-white page costs 2 lira and a colour page costs 6 lira. The printer is next to the kitchen on the second floor. Scanning to email is free.

## Mail and packages

You can use our address for business mail with any membership. Packages are kept at the front desk for up to seven days, after which they are returned to the sender.

## Lockers

Lockers cost 250 lira per month and are on the first floor. Bring your own padlock.

## Parking

There is no parking at the building. The nearest public car park is two streets away and members get a 20 percent discount there by showing their key card.
