# Kestrelwood Coworking: house rules

Kestrelwood Coworking is a made-up coworking space used to test this starter. Every name, price and address in these files is invented.

## Opening hours

The front desk is staffed from 08:30 to 18:00, Monday to Friday. Members with a resident desk can enter at any hour with their key card. On Saturdays the building opens from 10:00 to 16:00 for all members. The building is closed on Sundays and on public holidays.

## Guests

Members may bring up to two guests per day. Guests must sign in at the front desk and wear a visitor badge. A guest who stays longer than four hours is charged a day pass.

## Quiet zone

The third floor is a quiet zone. Phone calls are not allowed there; use the phone booths on the second floor instead. Keyboards with loud mechanical switches should stay on the first floor.

## Kitchen

Coffee and tea are free for members. Label food in the shared fridge with your name and date. The fridge is emptied every Friday at 17:00, and anything left inside is thrown away.

## Pets

Dogs are welcome on the first floor only, on a lead, and must not be left alone. Other pets are not allowed.

## Lost key cards

A lost key card is blocked as soon as you report it. A replacement card costs 150 lira and is ready the next working day.
